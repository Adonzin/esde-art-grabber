**Fix: Android apps getting art from unrelated games**

The Chrome browser could get the background of the 2003 PC game *Chrome*. Apps that the grabber couldn't link to an installed app were searched as games.

- Android apps and emulators are now **never** looked up on SteamGridDB, Steam or Libretro.
- Apps are now also found by their name ("Chrome" → Google Chrome), so entries made by ES-DE's own Game importer get their real icon too.
- Apps that aren't installed on the device are skipped, and the summary says how many.
- New **Clear game art from apps…** button (under *What to download*): lists the backgrounds and other game images older versions put on your apps and emulators, and removes them after you confirm.

**To clean up wrong art:** tap *Clear game art from apps…*, then select only the *androidapps* and *emulators* systems, tick *Replace art that already exists* and run once. Untick it afterwards.
