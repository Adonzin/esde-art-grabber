**Moonlight categories fill themselves from your PC**

- In a Moonlight category, tap **Get games from my PC…**. The app finds PCs running **Sunshine** or **Apollo** on your network (or you type the address), you log in once with your Sunshine settings username and password, and it lists every app your PC streams. Tick the ones you want.
- The computer's name is filled in automatically, exactly as Moonlight shows it, so launching works first time.
- **Refresh from PC** (category menu) picks up apps you've added on the PC since.
- Passwords are only remembered if you tick *Remember*, and only ever sent to PCs on your home network. Sunshine's security certificate is remembered the first time; if it ever changes, the app won't send your password and tells you why.
