**New art sources**
- **Libretro thumbnails**: real box art, screenshots, title screens and logos for retro consoles, matched by ROM name and region. No key needed.
- **Official Steam art**: Steam games get their own Steam cover, logo and background. Games from other stores that are also on Steam can get it too.
- SteamGridDB fills in whatever the others don't have. Each source can be switched on or off under **Sources**, and SteamGridDB can be turned off entirely (then no API key is needed).

**Update app button**
- The new **App updates** card checks this page for a newer version and installs it for you.

Install over your current version; settings are kept.
