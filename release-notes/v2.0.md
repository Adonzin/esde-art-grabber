**ES-DE Art Grabber is now ES-DE Commander**

It still does everything the art grabber did, and adds a **Categories** screen for making your own ES-DE systems and collections without touching XML or folders:

- **Android app categories:** make systems like *Streaming*, *Tools* or *Social* by ticking installed apps. They launch exactly like ES-DE's own Android Apps, and the art grabber gives them their app icons.
- **Moonlight category:** enter your PC's name and your games' names as Moonlight shows them, and each entry opens that game directly in Moonlight.
- **Collections:** group entries you already have, and optionally show each collection as its own item on ES-DE's main screen.
- Choose which existing system's look a category borrows from your theme.
- Systems, rules and collections you made yourself are never changed, and ES-DE's own system names can't be reused.
- Every change is backed up first, and **Undo last change** puts everything back.

Close ES-DE before making changes; they appear the next time you open it.

Installs over your current version; settings are kept.
