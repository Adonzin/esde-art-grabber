**Custom looks for your categories**

ES-DE themes only have artwork for ES-DE's own systems. Now your categories can have their own:

- **Themes screen:** finds the theme ES-DE uses and makes an **editable copy**, *"<Theme> (ESCommander)"*, downloaded from the same place ES-DE's theme downloader uses. Your original theme isn't touched. **Refresh copy** gets the latest version and puts your looks back.
- **Custom look** (in each category's menu): pick a main picture, a logo and a background, each from a picture file or an **installed app's icon**. App icons on big tiles sit centred on a colour you choose.
- The app works out where your theme keeps each system's pictures and info, and saves yours there at the right sizes and formats. That includes SVG logo slots and the theme's own customisation folders. Tested with PS5 Menu, Linear, Modern, Slate, Canvas, Iconic, Art Book Next and NSO Menu.
- Your category also gets its own name and description in themes that show system info.
- Works for collections too, in themes that give collections their own artwork (set *Group custom collections* to *Never*).
- Files the theme already has are never overwritten, and removing a look removes exactly what it added.

Then choose the ESCommander theme in ES-DE under *UI Settings → Theme*.
